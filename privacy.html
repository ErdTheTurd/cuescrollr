Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Cuescrollr (“the App”) is published by Edward Dunn.

Summary

Cuescrollr is offline-only. The App does not collect or transmit personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. Scripts and settings stay on your device.

What the App does

Cuescrollr is a teleprompter for iPhone Duo and iPhone. You store scripts on the device, scroll them while presenting, and — when you choose — use the front camera as a local presenter preview.

Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service that sends data off the device.

On-device storage

Scripts and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes that local data according to iOS behavior.

Camera

The front camera is used only as a local presenter preview so you can check framing while you read. Cuescrollr does not record the camera preview and does not upload photos or video.

Network

Cuescrollr does not require a network connection for its features and does not contact remote servers as part of normal use.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not set cookies or collect information about you.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@cuescrollr.com or see the support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at this URL.
