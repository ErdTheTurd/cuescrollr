Two ways to present

Same teleprompter, shaped to the device in your hand.

iPhone Duo

The script scrolls on one screen while the other shows a local front-camera preview and your controls. Read and check framing without flipping the device.

iPhone

The script overlays the camera view. Controls sit in a panel so you can play, pause, and adjust without leaving the take.

Built for the read

Everything you need to deliver cleanly — nothing that pulls you online.

  • Script library Keep scripts on the device and open the one you need.
  • Smooth auto-scroll Steady motion at a pace you set in words per minute.
  • Play and pause Start, stop, and resume when you need a beat.
  • Speed and font size Dial WPM and type size to match the room and the lens.
  • Mirror Flip the script when you’re reading from a mirrored display.
  • 3-2-1 countdown A short count before the scroll begins.
  • Reading guide A guide line keeps your eye on the current phrase.
  • Works offline No account and no network required for the app’s features.

It stays on the phone

Cuescrollr is offline-only. Scripts and settings never leave the device. The front camera is used only as a local presenter preview — it is not recorded or uploaded. Read the privacy policy.
