Published by Edward Dunn

Cuescrollr is a teleprompter for iPhone Duo and iPhone. On iPhone Duo, the script can scroll on one screen while the other shows a local front-camera preview and controls. On a regular iPhone, the script overlays the camera and controls sit in a panel.

A few things worth knowing

Does Cuescrollr need the internet?

No. The app works offline. Scripts and settings stay on your device.

Why does the app ask for the camera?

The front camera is used only as a local presenter preview so you can check framing while you read. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Where are my scripts stored?

In a script library on the device. Uninstalling the app removes local data according to iOS behavior.

What can I adjust while presenting?

Play and pause, scroll speed (WPM), font size, mirror, a 3-2-1 countdown, and a reading guide line.

Is the app on the App Store yet?

Not yet. This site will link to the App Store when Cuescrollr is available.
